The desktop build loads dependency JARs from this directory.

h2-1.4.200.jar is H2 version 1.4.200.
jbcrypt-upstream.jar is a local Java 8 build of org.mindrot.jbcrypt.BCrypt.
The upstream source and its license notice are included in the JAR.
Source: https://github.com/djmdjm/jBCrypt/blob/master/src/org/mindrot/jbcrypt/BCrypt.java

For a fresh dependency install, the corresponding Maven artifacts are
com.h2database:h2:1.4.200 and org.mindrot:jbcrypt:0.4.
